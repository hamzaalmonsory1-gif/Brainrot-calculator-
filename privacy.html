Brainrot Calculator — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Brainrot Calculator handles information when you use the bot on Discord.

2. Information We Process

Brainrot Calculator may process information provided by Discord that is necessary for the bot to operate. This may include Discord User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, usernames, and information related to commands used with the bot.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the bot is used only to provide, maintain, and improve its features, including processing commands and calculations.

4. Data Storage

Brainrot Calculator does not intentionally store personal information such as passwords, email addresses, private messages, or payment information.

If the bot stores server settings, user preferences, or other information required for a feature, that information is stored only for the purpose of providing that feature and is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user information to third parties. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the bot or comply with applicable laws.

6. Discord

Brainrot Calculator operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Removal Requests

If you believe the bot has stored information associated with you or your server and you would like it removed, contact the developer through Discord.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handled by Brainrot Calculator, contact the developer through Discord.
